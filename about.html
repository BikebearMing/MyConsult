About Us

Myconsult Sdn Bhd is an accounting software and outsourced accounting firm based in Kota Damansara, Selangor, founded in 2003.

For its first decade the company was an MYOB (now ABSS) dealer. Since 2013 it has been an authorised dealer for both AutoCount and SQL Accounting, completing over 1,000 implementations for Malaysian SMEs.
Today the business runs two divisions: implementing and supporting accounting software, and handling accounting and payroll on clients’ behalf. The team also works daily in Xero and QuickBooks on outsourced engagements.

Why choose Myconsult

Most dealers sell one system, so their recommendation is decided before they meet you. We have implemented across three generations of accounting software, and our accounting team uses several more every day. That means we can tell you which system fits your business, even when the answer is one we do not sell.

Three things follow from that

We recommend on
trajectory, not features.

If your needs are basic and stable, SQL Accounting does the job. If you plan to add outlets, stock complexity or headcount, AutoCount saves you a migration later.

We have done the
migrations.

Moving from one accounting system to another is a fresh setup, not a conversion. We sold and supported MYOB/ABSS for ten years before moving clients off it, so we know where each system’s ceiling is.

We use the software,
not just sell it.

Our outsourced accounting team works in these systems every month. Our implementations are set up the way accountants actually need them.
