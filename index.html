Frequently Asked
Questions.

Should I choose AutoCount or SQL Accounting?

It depends on how your business sells. Retail and F&B businesses selling direct to consumers are usually better served by AutoCount, because of its multi-outlet handling and integrated POS. Service businesses and those with straightforward needs are usually better served by SQL Accounting. Both come in an on-premise and a cloud version. Myconsult is an authorised dealer for both, so we recommend based on fit rather than on which product we carry.

How much does AutoCount or SQL Accounting cost in Malaysia?

A single-company SQL Accounting package starts from RM1,499, which includes the software and half a day of basic training. AutoCount starts from RM2,499. Full implementation, covering database setup, 12 to 15 hours of training, 5 customised forms and your first year of support, starts from RM2,000 on top of the software.

How long does an accounting software implementation take?

A basic setup takes one to two weeks, and most single-company implementations go live in three to four weeks. Complex projects with multiple companies or system customisation run up to two months. Larger projects with multiple outlets, POS integration and a full migration can run longer: a six-outlet retail implementation we delivered took about three months.

What makes an implementation take longer than expected?

Two things, most often. Form customisation is open-ended unless the scope is agreed upfront. And database setup depends on the client: the project waits while opening balances, master data and sign-off come across. That is why we scope and price implementation as its own piece of work.

What is included in outsourced accounting?

We collect your documents, maintain the books, and prepare your accounts monthly, bi-monthly or quarterly. Accounts come with a Myconsult HealthCheck report, a plain-language read of your balance sheet and income statement. Outsourced accounting starts from RM800 per month for a single company with straightforward transaction volume.

Do you handle payroll as well as accounting?

Yes. Payroll starts from RM350 per month, including EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB submissions, and can be taken with or without outsourced accounting.

Is Myconsult HealthCheck included?

It is included for outsourced accounting clients on more than RM1,500 per month, and available for RM100 per month on smaller plans. AutoCount and SQL Accounting users can also request a free one-off HealthCheck.

What does e-Invoice setup involve?

We link your accounting system to the MyInvois portal, set up your documents so everything requiring e-Invoice submission carries the QR code, and run one-on-one training with your team. For new AutoCount and SQL Accounting clients this is included in the implementation; adding it to an existing system costs RM2,000.

Can you handle e-Invoice submission for us?

For outsourced accounting clients, we handle self-billed e-Invoice submission each month, quoted together with your accounting fee.

Can you support AutoCount or SQL Accounting bought from another dealer?

Yes. We take over support with a dealer change form, with no switching fee.
